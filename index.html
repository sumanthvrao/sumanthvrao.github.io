I am a PhD student in the CSE Department at UC San Diego. I'm fortunate to be advised by Stefan Savage and Geoff Voelker.

I study cybersecurity problems through large-scale empirical measurement. My work spans threats to the systems we rely on every day: the security of email systems (Oakland'26, WWW'24), aftermarket automotive remote-control systems (USENIX Security'26), and the impact of large-scale IT outages on hospital patient care (JAMA'25). I'm a member of the Sysnet and CryptoSec research groups at UC San Diego. My hometown is Bangalore (ಬೆಂಗಳೂರು), India.

I am on the 2026-27 job market, seeking postdoc and industry research positions in security. CV Resume
